01 — What it is

An in-app
point

You earn it by using the app and you spend it inside the app. That is the entire definition — there is nothing behind it, and nothing more to it.

It is our way of thanking you for being here, and of giving you a reason to come back tomorrow. Every message you send and every sentence you correct is what keeps this place alive. The token is what that adds up to. Everyone starts with 250.

The daily pool

On top of what you score directly, a fixed pool is shared out at the end of every day among everyone who was active, in proportion to how active they were. Your share depends on everyone else’s day as well as your own — and nobody can take more than 5% of it, 250 tokens.

04 — Returning

Coming
back?

÷ 100 Old balance, new scale

Your balance carries over. Nothing on LangX was ever bought or sold — what looked like a purchase log was really the daily payout being calculated — so every token in your balance was earned, and there is no reason not to honour it.

Balances are divided by 100 on the way in, and we would rather say that plainly than let you find it out afterwards. Old balances run as high as 2.28 million, while a very active day now is about 700 tokens. Credited one-for-one, the largest balance would sit roughly nine years ahead of anyone new and the all-time board would never move again. Divided, it starts about a month ahead — a real head start, and one someone else can still close.

You also get a welcome-back bonus of 250, and the streak you had is frozen rather than lost: you can spend tokens to bring it back. Sign in with the same email address you used before — here is how.

05 — Questions

Straight answers

Is LangX Token a cryptocurrency?

No. It is not on a blockchain, there is no contract and no wallet address, and it is not listed on any exchange. It is a number in your LangX account, earned in the app and spent in the app.

Does LangX Token have a price?

No. It has no price and no cash value, because there is nowhere to buy or sell it. A coin with a similar name on a price-tracking site is not LangX Token.

Can I buy, sell or withdraw tokens?

No. Tokens cannot be bought with money or anything else, sent to another member, or taken out of the app. They cannot unlock Fluent or Polyglot either — those are subscriptions.

How many tokens can I earn in a day?

Messages pay up to 200 a day, Echo up to 25, and a share of the daily pool up to 250. Corrections, pronunciation answers and new conversations have no daily cap. A very active day comes to about 700 tokens.

What happened to my tokens from the old LangX app?

They carried over, divided by 100, and you get a welcome-back bonus of 250 on top. Sign in with the same email address you used before.

Does spending tokens lower my leaderboard rank?

No. The leaderboards rank tokens earned, not tokens held. Spending lowers your balance, never your all-time total.
